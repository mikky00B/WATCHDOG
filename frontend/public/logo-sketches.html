WATCHDOG — logo sketches

Six working marks. Every card shows the pairing with the wordmark, then a favicon test (32px / 16px), a dark-mode version and a one-color version. Judge them at 16px — that is where they live.

WATCHDOG

A — Eartipped W

The recommendation. One continuous stroke: the outer strokes of the W bend outward at the top and become ears, the middle peak stays clean. Nothing to clean up at small sizes because there is nothing extra.
